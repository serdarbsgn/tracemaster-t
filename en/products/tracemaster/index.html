TraceMaster Machines

Smart PPE and consumable management vending machines specially designed for industrial production environments.

TraceTurn

TraceTurn is a smart vending machine where materials that are difficult to load into spiral machines can be stored.

Key Features

  • Vending machine suitable for industrial production environments
  • Web-based and user-friendly interface
  • Easy and fast loading
  • Various usage possibilities by dispensing and returning materials
  • Easy-to-use touch and wide screen
  • Material dispensing via ID badge
  • Individual and group-specific material limits
  • Easily adjustable material compartments
  • Operates with a carousel system
  • Storage capacity for 12 different materials
  • Can connect to the system via Ethernet cable, Wi-Fi, and SIM card

Reports

  • Loading time and Machine stock movements
  • Stock and order status
  • Report showing dispensings and returns based on machine, product, brand, and cost center
  • Report of unreturned materials

TraceBox

A 16-compartment accessory PPE cabinet that allows for the dispensing of bulkier PPE products such as shoes, helmets, clothing, and equipment.

Key Features

  • Vending machine suitable for industrial production environments
  • Preferred especially for the storage and tracking of bulky materials
  • Web-based and user-friendly interface
  • Easy and fast loading
  • Has 16 independent compartments with doors
  • Devices placed in the compartments can be charged during storage
  • Easy-to-use touch and wide screen
  • Material dispensing via ID badge
  • Ability to define individual and group-specific material limits
  • Instant notification for unreturned materials
  • Connection via Ethernet cable, Wi-Fi, and SIM card

Reports

  • Loading time
  • Machine stock movements
  • Stock and order status
  • Reports based on machine, product, brand, and cost center
  • Report of unreturned materials

TraceLocker-30

A 30-compartment accessory PPE cabinet that allows for the dispensing of bulkier PPE products such as shoes, helmets, clothing, and equipment.

Key Features

  • Vending machine suitable for industrial production environments
  • Machine dimensions: 50 x 75 x 183 cm
  • Dimensions of each compartment: 45 x 30 x 9 cm
  • Ability to combine 4 compartments vertically
  • Preferred especially for the storage and tracking of bulky materials
  • Has 30 independent compartments with doors
  • Devices placed in the compartments can be charged during storage
  • Instant notification for unreturned materials
  • Connection via Ethernet cable, Wi-Fi, and SIM card

Reports

  • Machine stock movements
  • Stock and order status
  • Report showing dispensings and returns based on machine, product, brand, and cost center
  • Report of unreturned materials

TraceLocker-32

High security vending solution. Works in combination with the TraceTurn or TraceLocker-30 machine.

Key Features

  • Vending machine suitable for industrial production environments
  • Machine dimensions: 50 x 75 x 183 cm
  • Dimensions of each compartment: 45 x 30 x 9 cm
  • Ability to combine 4 compartments vertically
  • Has 32 independent compartments with doors
  • Devices placed in the compartments can be charged during storage
  • Original High Security Vending Solution
  • Can be combined with TraceTurn or TraceLocker-30 (up to 8 units max)
  • Connection via Ethernet cable, Wi-Fi, and SIM card

Reports

  • Machine stock movements
  • Stock and order status
  • Reports based on machine, product, brand, and cost center
  • Report of unreturned materials
